Install in 5 steps (takes 1 minute)

Download and unzipDownload the ZIP above. Right-click it → Extract All. You will get a folder named RexSnap.
Open the extensions pageIn Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter. (Edge: edge://extensions)
Turn on Developer modeSwitch on Developer mode (toggle at the top-right).
Load the extensionClick Load unpacked and select the extracted RexSnap folder (the one that contains manifest.json).
Pin it and use itClick the puzzle icon → pin Rex Snap. Open Google Meet, join, turn your camera on, then snap your fingers in front of the camera. (Backup: Alt + S or the floating 🫰 Snap button.)
⚠️ The first time, Chrome asks for microphone permission (only used to hear the snap sound; you can switch this off in the popup). Reload your meeting tab once after installing (and before turning the camera on) so the effect can attach to your camera. If you turn the camera on first, turn it off and on again.
